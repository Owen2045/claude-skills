→ No daily monitoring; check quarterly

### Retiree (needs cash flow)

→ Target yield: 3-4%

### Aggressive investor (willing to do research)
→ Aggressive template  
→ Answer the Three Questions before every entry

## Rebalancing Rules

1. **Trigger**: Any position drifts >5% from target weight (not calendar-based)
2. **Method**: Use new cash inflows to top up underweight positions; avoid selling overweight ones to minimize tax events
3. **Frequency cap**: Rebalance at most once every six months to prevent over-trading
4. **Bear market exception**: When the broad market drops >20%, deploy the cash buffer to add to core positions

---

## FX Risk for Taiwan-Based Investors

- Holding US stocks is structurally a long-USD position
- When TWD appreciates: USD-denominated returns are eroded when converted back to NTD
- When TWD depreciates: USD-denominated returns are amplified when converted back to NTD
- **Recommended approach**: No active FX hedging; long-term holding naturally averages out the cost basis
- **Practical note**: When converting currency, use the bank's spot rate and avoid products with wide bid-ask spreads
