# Taiwan Sector Analysis Framework

> This file is a reference supplement. Always search for current data before answering — do not rely on the stock names below as definitive current leaders.

## Cycle Assessment Method

### Four Key Indicators
1. **Monthly revenue YoY growth**: Three or more consecutive months of >20% YoY growth = strong momentum
2. **Gross margin trend**: Expanding margins = pricing power confirmed; the re-pricing cycle is real
3. **Institutional positioning**: Foreign + domestic funds buying together = institutional conviction
4. **Order visibility**: "Orders booked through H2" in earnings call = high near-term certainty

### Cycle Position Quick Table
| Indicator | Recovery | Growth | Peak | Correction |
|-----------|----------|--------|------|------------|
| Monthly revenue | Bottoming out | Consistently hitting highs | Plateauing | Declining |
| Gross margin | Recovering from lows | Expanding steadily | At highs | Compressing |
| Institutional stance | Starting to accumulate | Aggressively buying | Trimming gradually | Clear net-selling |
| Market sentiment | Nobody's watching | Starting to get attention | Everyone's chasing it | Panic selling |

## Entry Timing Reference

### Signals That Support Entry
- Price pulling back to test 20MA or 60MA support
- Revenue: consecutive MoM growth and accelerating YoY rate
- Institutions turning net-buy (foreign institutions net-buying 3+ consecutive days)
- Technical: KD indicator forming a golden cross from oversold levels

### Signals That Call for Standing Aside
- Price has surged >30% in a short period with no consolidation
- Stock placed on disposition watch (trading restrictions triggered)
- Post-earnings call: foreign institutions sharply cut target price
- Core product pricing shows signs of softening

### Stop-Loss Discipline
- Price drops 8-10% from entry point → exit unconditionally
- Sector fundamentals show structural deterioration → don't wait for the stop; exit immediately
- Original thesis invalidated → take the loss and move on; don't hold hoping to recover
